# Driver application shell

The Driver app starts after the shared Identity foundation. DriverProfile, compliance eligibility and explicit vehicle/service permissions remain separate from account authentication.

Phase 0.6 extends the assignment-to-pickup client with low-distraction active telemetry, authoritative health/reconnect state, persistent SOS, destination-approach evidence and governed completion. Required handover is shown as a hard blocker. Navigation, production device capture and authorised handover entry remain future work.

Phase 0.7 reads separately posted DriverEarning truth. The client never infers an earning from Journey completion or Rider fare, and an earning never implies payout.

Phase 0.8 adds safe application start/resume and a read-only operating-eligibility explanation. Contact verification can be recognised from authoritative Identity truth, while identity checks, documents, assessed competency, vehicle eligibility, service permission and approval remain separate. OCR cannot approve compliance, application approval does not itself grant operating eligibility, and availability remains a separate Driver action.

Phase 0.9 adds read-only Fleet Marketplace, current agreement and vehicle-assignment validation surfaces. Supplier terms must be current and verified; deposits are not revenue; capabilities are explicit; actual-pair insurance and replacement checks fail closed. Reservation, agreement acceptance, deposit deduction and assignment commands remain unavailable.

Phase 0.10 adds maintenance and verified-perk reads plus an idempotent pre-shift observation. A Driver may report “something does not feel right” without diagnosis; the concern stops vehicle use for precautionary review and creates no Driver fault finding. Repair, provider booking, return-to-service and replacement fulfilment remain authorised operational workflows and are not exposed here.

Phase 0.11 adds a fair-treatment projection with separate rating, complaint, finding, restriction, appeal and Driver-protection dimensions and explicitly no opaque Driver Score. Drivers can report Rider conduct, safely terminate an unsafe active Journey with rating protection and passenger continuity, submit a high-impact decision appeal, and view only current Finance-approved incentive truth. Driver self-service cannot adjudicate a complaint, decide an appeal, apply a restriction or publish incentive terms.

Phase 0.12 adds the complete daily-operations projection, normal BREAK/FINISHING_SOON controls, weak-signal reconciliation, informed-offer disclosure, chosen-pickup arrival-plan truth, capability-based supply labels and typed Driver Support. Blind offer acceptance fails closed until pickup ETA and a Finance-approved Driver-earning estimate exist. Reconciliation never pretends queued critical events were executed, OFFLINE stops ordinary app location, and high-risk active support requires human escalation.

Phase 0.13 adds the same authenticated communication inbox/read/acknowledgement client as Rider. Safety, Driver operations, payments, support and marketing stay purpose-separated; critical acknowledgement failure remains an explicit human-escalation condition. External delivery providers are disabled, so queued intent is never shown as delivered.

Phase 0.14 adds provider-disabled telephone capability, Contact Plan and Driver-owned interaction-history clients. Telephone routing cannot bypass Driver/Journey/Safety/Finance authority, caller ID is not identity, active context can follow a warm handoff, dropped calls remain idempotent and raw card details never enter operator/general-voice flows.

Phase 0.15 adds Driver-owned Contact Centre case and communications-assurance reads. Driver, Fleet, compliance, payout and Support facts remain role-scoped to their canonical owners; critical failure becomes owned casework; channel history survives handoff; contactability never becomes a Driver rating; and no provider or staff mutation is enabled.
